# Three worked patterns

These are starting shapes. Adapt every number to the real story and assets.

## Scattered cards become one view

A 15 second product video at 1920 by 1080 and 30 frames a second, 450 frames in all:

| Seconds | Frames | What the scene does |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | 0 to 89 | Separate cards show the problem |
| 3 to 7 | 90 to 209 | A cursor gathers the cards into one view |
| 7 to 12 | 210 to 359 | The overview settles and the benefit can be read |
| 12 to 15 | 360 to 449 | A closing invitation holds |

Use an invented, clearly labeled concept or the real approved interface. In Remotion, a spring with mass 1, stiffness 180 and damping 22 is a reasonable first value, not a rule. Check the cursor targets and landing positions near seconds 3 and 7. A portrait version needs its own layout.

## One shape carries several interface states

One container moves through eight to twelve states: a button, loading, success, a player, a slider, a toggle, tabs, a chart, search, a toast. Use controlled springs, one accent color or none, and a cursor action to cause each change. Separate the drag movement from the spring that settles afterwards. Pick the states the real product has.

Use the renderer's supported path for motion blur and seeking. Do not add a second renderer for one effect.

## Music sets the timeline

Analyze the supplied recording. Choose an excerpt that is musically complete. Keep the offset into the source, and convert beat and word timings to composition time. Assign scene changes and accents to those times, then check the sync in the export, not only in the preview.

Keep three things apart in your notes: the style of the music, its nominal tempo, and what you measured. Tell an intended fade apart from a cut made only to hit a duration.

## Paid work, only when asked

Start from a credible sample and a bounded offer: how many videos, which formats, how many revision rounds, whether source files are included, and what counts as accepted. Price from the real work. Other people's earnings stories are not rates. Research and drafting are separate from sending anything.
