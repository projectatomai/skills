# Design direction first

Do not build a design on a guess. Before a mock-up, a canvas or front-end code, either find the direction the person has already given, or ask for the part that is missing. Skip this only when they say "just go" or have already picked a direction for this project.

## Step 1: Read what already exists

Look for the things that must be kept: the logo, colors, fonts, the design system, any written design rules in the project, and references the person approved earlier. List them back as "keeping these", so they do not have to repeat themselves.

When a direction is already set, work inside it and go straight to the work. A direction the person has supplied is approval to build within it.

## Step 2: Ask only for what is missing

For a direction that is new or still open, ask in one short message, with quick options so the answer can be a few words:

1. **References.** One to three sites or screenshots they like, or a pick from the galleries below. This matters most: a real reference beats any amount of description.
2. **Mood.** For example playful and loud, calm and editorial, technical and dense, premium and minimal, retro, or brutalist.
3. **Light or dark**, and any colors that must or must not be used.
4. **Type.** For example a big serif, condensed poster type, a clean grotesk, mono or pixel.
5. **Motion.** None, subtle, or a centerpiece.
6. **Anything they dislike** or have seen too often.

When the direction is wide open, show three low-fidelity mock-ups that really differ from each other, side by side. Let the person pick one or combine them, and wait for that choice before you build production UI.

## Step 3: Design from the answers

- Pull concrete traits from each reference (layout, type, color, motion), name them back in a line or two, then design.
- Avoid the default AI-site look unless it is asked for: a near-black background, a big grotesk headline with one accent color, mono labels such as "01 / SECTION", rounded cards in a grid, and a glow or globe hero.
- Use placeholders such as [N] or [$X.XX] where you do not have a real number. Do not invent figures.
- Keep the project's existing components. Record the approved decisions in the project's design notes when they will guide later work.

## Step 4: Show it and check it

Show the real render, with two lines on which reference drove which choice, and ask what to push further. Before you call it done, compare the render with the references and look for the generic patterns listed above.
