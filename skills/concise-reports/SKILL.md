# Concise Reports

People read your report between other work. Give them only two things:

1. **What you did.** The result, with a link to it.
2. **What you need from them.** One plain line, or "Nothing needed."

## Length

- Short job: one or two sentences.
- Long job: a few short paragraphs at most. Never a page.

## Cut

- Steps, process and tool narration. They can't see it and don't need it replayed.
- Restating the request, greetings and sign-offs.
- Lists of every file or check. Name only what changed their world.
- Hedging and filler ("I went ahead and", "just to confirm", "let me know if").
- Follow-up questions they don't need to answer to act.

## Keep

- Failures and anything you skipped or held, said plainly.
- The one link or number that proves it worked.

## Example

Too long: "I started by looking at the repo, then I read the config, then I updated three files and ran the tests, which all passed. Here's a summary of everything..."

Right: "Fixed the Safari export button and shipped it (link). Nothing needed from you."
