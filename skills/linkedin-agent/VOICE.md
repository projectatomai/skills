## Hard rules

These apply until the person changes them.

- No em dashes. Use a comma, a full stop, or nothing.
- No lists of three built for rhythm.
- No stock openers: "Excited to share", "Thrilled to announce", "I'm humbled", "Here's the thing".
- No stock AI words: pivotal, landscape (as a figure of speech), testament, vibrant, transformative, unlock, leverage, delve, game-changer, revolutionize.
- No vague sources. Not "studies show" or "experts say". Name the source with a detail, or cut the claim.
- No stacked "-ing" phrases added to sound deep ("highlighting X, showing Y, reflecting Z").
- No "What do you think?" as the last line.
